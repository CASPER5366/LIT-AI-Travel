FINAL NOTICE — LOGOS & BRANDING
================================
Place your logo image files in this folder, then upload via:
Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes > Customize

FILES TO ADD HERE:
------------------
circular-badge-logo.png
  → The round badge: "FINAL NOTICE" top / skull / "DEATH COMES FOR US ALL" bottom
  → Upload to: Theme Customize > Header > Logo image (PRIMARY LOGO)

banner-logo.png
  → Hooded skull above "FINAL NOTICE" banner ribbon
  → Upload to: Theme Customize > Header > Logo image (ALTERNATIVE)

skull-icon.png
  → Standalone hooded skull face (no text)
  → Upload to: Theme Customize > Favicon
  → Also use as: product card watermark / social avatar

RECOMMENDED IMAGE SPECS:
  Logo: PNG with transparent background, min 400px wide
  Favicon: PNG square, 32x32 or 64x64px
